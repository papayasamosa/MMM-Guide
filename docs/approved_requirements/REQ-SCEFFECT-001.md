## Requirement

### 1. Jointly estimated mediation where identification and engine support permit

Where an approved graph identifies an observed-variable mediation or
multi-node path structure, and the selected engine's resolved capability
(`REQ-SCENGINE-001` §2) supports the required likelihood, hierarchy,
temporal structure, and coverage pattern, that structure may be estimated
jointly as a Bayesian structural system — mediator and outcome equations
sharing one posterior — rather than only as separate, independently
fitted stages. This is permissive ("may"), not mandatory: a supported
sequential-posterior-propagation pattern (`AGENTS.md`'s "Valid estimation
patterns") remains an acceptable alternative where joint estimation is not
supported or not chosen.

### 2. Full-link, posterior-intervention effect evaluation for non-linear/count outcomes

For a non-linear or count outcome (the fitted count model's log link, per
`AGENTS.md`'s "Mathematical rules"), an official direct, mediated, or
total structural effect produced by a structural causal engine must be
evaluated on the outcome scale through posterior intervention — comparing
`mu`/prediction under an intervened plan against a reference plan across
posterior draws — and must never be represented solely as a product of
path coefficients on the linear predictor scale. This extends
`AGENTS.md`'s existing "Curves and economics" outcome-scale requirement
and `core.AGENTS.md`'s "Direct, mediated, and constrained-effect
reconciliation" section to a structural-causal-engine-produced effect,
without weakening either for the existing primary MMM or Candidate A.

### 3. Declared endogenous response

Any structural intervention result must declare which downstream
endogenous variables were allowed to respond to the intervention and
which were held fixed. A mediator variable must respond according to the
approved graph's mediation structure, not be silently held constant by
default when the requested estimand is a total effect, and not be
independently forecast in place of being generated by the model
(`AGENTS.md`'s "Future-variable roles" rule 4).

### 4. Reconciliation and no double counting

Where a structural engine produces direct, mediated, and total effects
for the same intervention, they must reconcile under the approved effect
definition (`total = direct + mediated` under the specified constraints,
mirroring `core.AGENTS.md`'s existing reconciliation contract), posterior-
draw-level before any summary statistic (`AGENTS.md`'s "Posterior
aggregation"), and must not double-count against Candidate A's own direct/
mediated/capacity-constrained contribution or any cross-product halo
component already represented elsewhere.

### 5. Candidate A is not replaced

This record does not authorise replacing Candidate A's own Search
mediation/capacity engine (`REQ-SEARCH-002`) with a generic structural-
causal-engine mediation graph. Candidate A's latent-demand/capacity
formulation remains the approved implementation for its authorised Search
structure regardless of whether a structural causal engine is later
adopted for other pathways.

### 6. Separate evidence dimension from identification

A structural intervention effect's *validity* (whether the estimand is
identified) is governed by `REQ-IDENT-001` (graphical identification) and
`REQ-LATENT-001` (latent-state identification), not by this record. This
record governs only how an already-identified structural effect is
*calculated* and *reconciled*. A posterior-intervention effect calculated
under this contract carries no implied identification status of its own.

  `UX-031`/`UX-032`);

  for when a structural effect may be reported officially.
